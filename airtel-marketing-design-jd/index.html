Senior Manager, Marketing Design

Set the creative bar for Airtel's marketing design, and lead the team that holds it there.

Brand & MarketingGurugram, IndiaFull-time8+ yearsPortfolio required

About the role

We are hiring a Senior Manager for Marketing Design to own the quality of what goes out. You will set the standard for how Airtel is expressed across marketing, from a launch film to a push notification, and lead the designers and partners who make it. This is a craft-first role. Your time goes into ideas, art direction and the work itself, not into moving deliverables through a queue.

What you'll do

  • Own the creative and visual quality bar for marketing design, and hold it when the timeline argues otherwise.
  • Art-direct Airtel's biggest moments, from brand campaigns and product launches to sponsorships, from first idea to final frame.
  • Turn dense propositions across pricing, network and bundled services into visual ideas that land in one pass.
  • Build the system underneath the work: design languages, kits and templates that let many teams ship on-brand without routing every file through you.
  • Work across brand, growth, product and marketing so the experience holds together on every surface a customer touches.
  • Lead and mentor designers. Hire well, give specific feedback, and build judgement rather than output.
  • Brief and steer agencies and production partners where we use them, then hold them to the standard you set.
  • Use performance data and creative experiments to sharpen the next round instead of guessing at it.
  • Put AI, motion and emerging tools to work where they genuinely raise quality or buy back time.

What we're looking for

  • Eight or more years in design, in-house or agency, with work you would still put in front of people today.
  • Exceptional craft in type, composition, colour and motion. You see the half-pixel, and you know when it does not matter.
  • Conceptual strength. You start from an idea rather than a layout, and you can defend it without a deck.
  • Range across digital, social, CRM, product surfaces, retail, OOH and video, with a view on what each medium actually needs.
  • Experience managing designers, and the ability to hold your position with senior stakeholders through reasoning rather than volume.
  • Comfort switching between brand work built to last years and performance work that ships this week, without dropping the bar on either.
  • A portfolio that shows thinking, not only finished frames.
